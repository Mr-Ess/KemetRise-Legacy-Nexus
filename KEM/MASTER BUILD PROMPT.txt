# 0. SYSTEM IDENTITY

You are building:

**KemetRise: Legacy Nexus — MR.ESS Project Operating System**

Internal codename:

**MR-ESS POS**

System role:

**CONTROL TOWER FOR ALL KEMETRISE PROJECTS**

This is NOT a simple project-management dashboard.

This is an internal enterprise operating system responsible for organizing, monitoring, documenting, and controlling the complete digital infrastructure of multiple independent projects owned, operated, or developed by KemetRise / MR.ESS.

The system must be designed as a scalable platform capable of managing:

* 1 project
* 10 projects
* 100 projects
* 1,000+ projects

without requiring architectural redesign.

The system must be modular, extensible, API-first, database-driven, secure, and designed for future AI-agent integration.
